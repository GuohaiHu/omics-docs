#  Trizol Tissue RNA Extraction

## Overview

Trizol method is the most classic and widely used total RNA extraction method in laboratories, based on the **guanidinium-phenol-chloroform single-step** principle, particularly suitable for animal tissue samples.

## Principle

1. **Lysis and RNase inactivation**: Guanidinium isothiocyanate rapidly lyses cells and completely inactivates RNase
2. **Phase separation**: After adding chloroform and centrifugation, three layers form:
   - Upper aqueous phase: contains RNA
   - Middle layer: contains DNA
   - Lower organic phase: contains proteins
3. **RNA precipitation**: Isopropanol precipitates RNA from the aqueous phase
4. **Washing and purification**: 75% ethanol wash removes salts
5. **Dissolution**: RNase-free water dissolves RNA

## Applicable Samples

- Fresh animal tissues (liver, brain, muscle, heart, etc.)
- Liquid nitrogen snap-frozen tissues
- Cultured cell pellets

## Characteristics

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Advantages** | Wide applicability, low cost, can simultaneously isolate DNA/RNA/protein |
| **Disadvantages** | Toxic reagents (phenol/chloroform), many steps, requires skill |
| **Extraction time** | ~1–1.5 hours |
| **Typical RIN** | ≥ 7.0 (fresh tissue) |

## Key Notes

-  **Keep cold**: Post-lysis steps on ice or at 4°C
-  **RNase-free environment**: DEPC water, RNase-free consumables, change gloves frequently
-  **Avoid middle layer**: Do not aspirate the protein interphase
-  **Do not over-dry**: Over-dried RNA pellets are difficult to dissolve

## Tissue-Specific Considerations

| Tissue Type | Special Challenge | Recommendation |
|-------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Liver** | Abundant RNase and glycogen | Increase lysis buffer, thorough homogenization |
| **Brain** | High lipid content | Additional chloroform extraction step |
| **Muscle** | Abundant connective tissue | Mince thoroughly or liquid nitrogen grinding |
| **Adipose** | Fat interference | Post-lysis 4°C incubation, remove upper fat layer |

## RNA Quality Assessment

| Metric | Normal Range | Significance |
|--------|-------------|--------------|
| **RIN** | ≥ 7.0 (ideal ≥ 8.0) | Integrity; < 6.0 not recommended for RNA-seq |
| **A260/A280** | 1.8 – 2.1 | Protein/phenol contamination |
| **A260/A230** | 2.0 – 2.2 | Carbohydrate/thiocyanate contamination |
| **Agarose gel** | 28S/18S ≈ 2:1 | Eukaryotic RNA should show clear double bands |
