#  DNA and RNA Extraction Overview

Nucleic acid extraction (核酸提取) is the foundational step of all downstream molecular biology workflows. The quality and integrity of extracted DNA and RNA directly determine the reliability of sequencing data and analytical conclusions.

## Importance of Nucleic Acid Extraction

High-quality nucleic acids are essential for:
- Accurate variant calling and genotyping
- Reliable gene expression profiling
- Successful library construction and sequencing
- Reproducible research outcomes

## DNA vs RNA Key Differences

| Feature | DNA | RNA |
|---------|-----|-----|
| **Stability** | Stable, robust | Fragile, easily degraded |
| **Structure** | Double-stranded | Single-stranded |
| **Extraction Environment** | Standard | RNase-free required |
| **Quality Metric** | Integrity, purity | RIN, purity, integrity |
| **Storage** | -20°C long-term | -80°C, avoid freeze-thaw |

## Impact on Downstream Sequencing

Poor extraction quality can lead to:
- Low library yields and sequencing depth
- Biased representation of genomic regions
- Artifacts in variant detection
- Inaccurate quantification in expression analysis
