#  Magnetic Sheet Blood DNA Extraction

## Overview

Magnetic sheet / automated magnetic rod extraction is a fully automated upgrade of magnetic bead extraction, using magnetic rods or sheets in pre-filled reagent strips/plates to automatically complete lysis, binding, washing and elution.

## Principle

1. **Automated lysis**: Instrument automatically adds lysis buffer and lyses leukocytes at constant temperature
2. **Magnetic bead binding**: Magnetic beads adsorb DNA under magnetic field
3. **Automated washing**: Magnetic rod transfers beads to wash buffer for multiple washes
4. **Automated elution**: Beads are transferred to elution buffer to release purified DNA

## Applicable Samples

- EDTA anticoagulant whole blood
- Sodium citrate anticoagulant whole blood
- Large-scale cohort screening samples

## Characteristics

| Aspect | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Advantages** | Fully automated, high throughput (96/192 per batch), best consistency |
| **Disadvantages** | High equipment cost, low flexibility, difficult for special samples |
| **Extraction time** | ~30–60 min/batch |
| **Typical purity** | A260/A280 ≈ 1.8–2.0 |

## Common Equipment

| Equipment | Manufacturer | Throughput | Features |
|-----------|-------------|------------|----------|
| **KingFisher** series | Thermo Fisher | 96/24 well | Classic magnetic rod extractor, most widely used |
| **BioSprint** | Qiagen | 96 well | Stable with dedicated kits |
| **Nextractor** series | Domestic | 96 well | Cost-effective for local applications |

## Key Notes

-  Process blood samples as soon as possible after collection
-  Different anticoagulants require corresponding kit programs
-  Regularly calibrate magnetic rod position
