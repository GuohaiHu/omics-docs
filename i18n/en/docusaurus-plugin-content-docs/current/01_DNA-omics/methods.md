## Sequencing Technologies

### Illumina Sequencing
- Short-read, high-throughput platform
- Excellent for SNP and small indel detection
- Cost-effective for large-scale studies

### Long-Read Sequencing
- PacBio and Oxford Nanopore
- Detect structural variants and large indels
- Resolve repetitive regions

## Sample Preparation

1. **DNA Extraction**: Isolate high-quality genomic DNA
2. **Library Construction**: Fragment DNA and add adapters
3. **Quality Control**: Verify DNA integrity and concentration
4. **Sequencing**: Run on chosen platform

## Best Practices

- Maintain DNA quality (RIN > 7)
- Use consistent sample handling protocols
- Document all preprocessing steps
- Archive raw sequencing data
