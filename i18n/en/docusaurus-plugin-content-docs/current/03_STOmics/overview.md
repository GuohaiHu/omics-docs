# Spatial Omics Overview

## What is Spatial Omics?

Spatial Omics preserves spatial information while measuring molecular features, enabling analysis of tissue organization and cellular interactions.

## Key Platforms

- **Visium**: Spatial RNA-seq with 55 μm resolution
- **MERFISH**: Multiplexed fluorescence with subcellular resolution
- **ISS**: In situ sequencing with high resolution
- **IMC**: Imaging mass cytometry for proteins

## Advantages

1. **Preserve Tissue Architecture**: Maintain native context
2. **Identify Spatial Patterns**: Detect local molecular signatures
3. **Study Cell-Cell Interactions**: Proximity-based analysis
4. **Map Microenvironments**: Characterize tissue niches

## Applications

- Cancer microenvironment analysis
- Developmental tissue mapping
- Immune infiltration patterns
- Disease progression tracking
