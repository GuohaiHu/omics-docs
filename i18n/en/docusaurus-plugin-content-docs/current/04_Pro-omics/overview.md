# Proteomics Overview

## What is Proteomics?

Proteomics is the large-scale study of proteins, including their identification, quantification, interactions, and modifications.

## Key Technologies

- **Mass Spectrometry**: Accurate protein quantification
- **Western Blotting**: Targeted protein detection
- **Immunoassays**: Multiplexed protein measurement
- **Protein Arrays**: High-throughput screening

## Analysis Levels

### Expression Proteomics
- Measure protein abundance changes
- Compare conditions or tissues
- Identify biomarkers

### Structural Proteomics
- Determine protein structure
- Study protein-protein interactions
- Map protein complexes

### Functional Proteomics
- Analyze post-translational modifications
- Study enzymatic activity
- Track protein localization

## Applications

- Biomarker discovery
- Drug target identification
- Disease mechanism studies
- Therapeutic development
