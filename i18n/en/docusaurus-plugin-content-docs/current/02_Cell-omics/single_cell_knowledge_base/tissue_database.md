## V. ECM Components Detailed

### Collagen Types and Distribution
| Collagen Type | Major Distribution | Molecular Weight | Degrading Enzyme |
|---------------|--------------------|------------------|------------------|
| Collagen I | Skin, bone, tendon, organ interstitium | 300 kDa | Collagenase I, II, III |
| Collagen II | Cartilage, vitreous body | 300 kDa | Collagenase I, III |
| Collagen III | Blood vessels, skin, uterus, reticular fibers | 300 kDa | Collagenase I, II, III |
| Collagen IV | Basement membrane (all epithelia) | 540 kDa | Collagenase IV, Liberase |
| Collagen V | Cell surface, placenta | 300 kDa | Collagenase V |

### Other ECM Components
| Component | Function | Distribution | Degradation Method |
|-----------|----------|--------------|--------------------|
| Fibronectin | Cell adhesion | Widespread | Dispase, Trypsin |
| Laminin | Basement membrane structure | Basement membrane | Dispase, Collagenase IV |
| Elastin | Elastic recoil | Blood vessels, lung, skin | Elastase, difficult to degrade |
| Hyaluronic Acid | Water retention, lubrication | Widespread | Hyaluronidase |
| Proteoglycan | Signal transduction, structure | Cartilage, brain | Difficult to degrade, mechanical assistance needed |
