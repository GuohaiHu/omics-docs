## II. Enzyme Selection Logic

### Highly Fibrotic Tumor Tissue
- **First choice**: Collagenase I + Dispase II + DNase I
- **Reason**: Collagenase I efficiently degrades collagen I/III (main components of tumor stroma), Dispase assists in breaking down fibronectin, DNase prevents DNA-mediated cell aggregation

### Very Soft Brain Tissue
- **First choice**: Papain + DNase I (cold dissociation or mild 37°C)
- **Reason**: Brain tissue has low ECM content and fragile cells; Papain provides gentle non-specific hydrolysis to avoid over-digestion

### Vascular-Rich Tissue (Liver, Spleen)
- **First choice**: Collagenase IV + DNase I
- **Reason**: Collagenase IV specifically degrades basement membrane collagen IV, protecting vascular endothelial cells

### Adipose Tissue
- **First choice**: Collagenase III + Dispase
- **Reason**: Collagenase III is more effective against collagen types in adipose tissue; Dispase assists in separating stromal vascular fraction (SVF)

## III. Critical Control Points (CCP)

| CCP No. | Control Point | Monitoring Parameter | Alert Limit | Corrective Action |
|---------|---------------|----------------------|-------------|-------------------|
| CCP1 | Enzymatic digestion time | Timer | ±5 min | Terminate reaction immediately |
| CCP2 | Reaction temperature | Thermometer/water bath | 37±1°C | Adjust water bath temperature |
| CCP3 | Enzyme activity | Batch validation | Expiration/storage conditions | Replace with new batch |
| CCP4 | Ion concentration | Ca2+/Mg2+ addition | Per protocol | Supplement cofactors |
| CCP5 | Termination timing | Microscope observation | Clumping rate `Clumping rate <10%lt;10%` | Add stop buffer immediately |
